About this project

The Ikot La Salle transportation tracker for De La Salle University – Dasmariñas.

About this project

The proposed transportation system aims to improve the overall accessibility, convenience, and efficiency of the Ikot La Salle transportation service within De La Salle University–Dasmariñas. It seeks to provide a more organized and user-friendly transportation experience for students and other members of the university community. Through the development of an accessible transportation platform, the system aims to enhance the way passengers interact with and utilize the campus transportation service.

Members: Jores, Sanchez, Ahmed

Methodology

Graph Theory is used in mapping the campus. To create a physical layout of the campus and jeep’s routes inside.

Algorithms is used in calculating the arrival time. Will be running in the background to convert live location updates into estimated arrival times (ETAs).

Propositional Logic & Equivalence determine jeepney availability. The service is open only between 7:00 AM and 4:00 PM, excluding lunch break. Based on the current time, the system displays whether the service is open or closed.

Estimation of Travel Time

Time between stops was computed from timed trips of the university jeepney along its two official routes: Route A (Gate 3 to Gate 1) and Route B (Gate 1 to Gate 3). For each pair of consecutive stops, the travel time was recorded over several observations, and the arithmetic mean of those observations was used as the average travel time for that segment. The two directions were measured separately because their stop sequences differ. The estimated time of arrival (ETA) at a stop is then the sum of the average travel times of all remaining segments between the jeepney’s current position and that stop. If the jeepney is between two stops, the remaining time of the current segment is added to the averages of the segments that follow. The data is preliminary, since each segment has relatively few observations, and the estimates do not account for live traffic.
